## 🧾 Overview  
[*It’s Okay!*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It%27s_Okay!) is a **2023 South Korean coming-of-age comedy-drama** film co-written and directed by **Kim Hye-young** in her directorial debut.  
The film stars **Lee Re**, **Jin Seo-yeon**, and **Chung Su-bin**.  

The story follows **In-yeong**, a high school student who loses her mother and finds solace by joining her school’s art group.  
She hides in the art club’s basement and is later discovered by **Seol-ah**, a strict but warm-hearted art director — and together they form an unlikely friendship that helps them both heal.  

🎬 **Release date:** February 26, 2025 (South Korea)  
⏱️ **Runtime:** 1 hour 42 minutes  
🎭 **Genre:** Comedy · Drama  
🎞️ **Director:** Kim Hye-young
